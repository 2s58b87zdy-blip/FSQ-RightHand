FSQ COMMAND 1.0 RC4.13

PO TO FSQ PACKING LIST

- Upload a customer PO as PDF, Word, Excel, image or text.
- ATLAS extracts PO number, delivery data and every readable PO line.
- Generates a structured, editable FSQ packing list with packages, dimensions,
  net weight, gross weight and marking.
- Missing values are marked for human review instead of being invented.
- Packing lists require approval before print or Word download.
- Printed documents include the FSQ logo and saved company details in the footer.
- Company details are shared securely through the existing Commander state store.
- Includes the AIS recovery and geographic world map from RC4.12.
